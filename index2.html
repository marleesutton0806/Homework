Cybersecurity and Online Saftey

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the root of technology saftey. It protects businesses and just every day people from hackers, scams,and so much more. Technology is used every single day by so much of the worlds population, every one of those people at at risk of having personal information stolen right off of their devices that why cybersecurity is so important, It helps protect those people.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

Phishing

Phishing is when a fake email, text or website link to scam someone. These messages can look very real, even though they are fake.

Malware

Malware is hamful software that can be downloaded on to a persons device through false websites and opening 'infected' files.

Ways to Stay Safe Online

  • Use strong and different passwords
  • Turn on two-factor authentication
  • Keep programs and devices updated
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments
  • Check that websites are legitimate
